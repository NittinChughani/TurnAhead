DRAFT / PILOT CONDITIONS

Terms of use

TurnAhead is an open-source walk-in queue tool. These draft conditions require review by the responsible operator before production use.

Scope

TurnAhead is not a medical record system, appointment service or emergency response tool. Waiting estimates are approximate. Follow hospital staff instructions and do not rely solely on browser alerts.

Authorized use

Only authorized staff may manage queues or hospital settings. Keep credentials and patient QR links confidential. Do not misuse the service, enter clinical information, or access records outside your duties.

Availability

Connectivity, browser suspension, platform outages and free-service limits can interrupt operation. Hospitals need a manual fallback and tested recovery process. Do not use TurnAhead as the only means of managing urgent care.

Open source

The software is provided under the repository's MIT license. The responsible hospital must determine whether it is suitable for its workflow and applicable requirements.
