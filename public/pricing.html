COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Is TurnAhead free for patients?

Yes. Patient tracking, QR tickets and public queue information are not charged to patients.

What is included in implementation?

Agreed workspace configuration and onboarding. Extra customization and integrations require a separate quotation.

Is TurnAhead still open-source?

Yes. The MIT-licensed source remains available. These packages are for the managed service, implementation and support.

How do I pay or renew?

Payment arrangements and verification are handled separately after a commercial agreement. There is no checkout or automatic charge on this page.

Can I change plans?

Authorized organization owners can request plan changes and renewals. Changes are subject to review, and existing clinical workflows must be preserved.
