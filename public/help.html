Help & support

Find guidance for patients, reception staff and administrators.

How do I track my turn?Patient

Scan the QR on your receipt. Keep the link private and the page open. Ask reception if the QR is unavailable.

Why is my wait estimate changing?Patient

It is an estimate based on recent service durations and queue position. Pauses, recalls and different service durations change it.

Will alerts work when my phone is locked?Patient

Not reliably. Keep the tracking page visible and remain within reach of reception announcements.

How do I print a QR ticket?Reception

Issue a ticket, choose Print on the receipt and select your printer. Check paper width and margins with fictional data first.

What if I lose connection during a call?Reception

Stop issuing or calling. Reconnect and verify the ticket state before retrying. Follow the hospital's manual queue fallback if needed.

How do I set up a hospital?Admin

An authorized operator provisions the workspace. Configure departments, doctors, counters, staff and branding using the setup guide.

Who can see patient history?Admin

The designated hospital owner can view the private attendance archive. Staff access is limited to operational duties.

How do I export analytics?Admin

Open Analytics, choose a period and generate a report. CSV and PDF contain aggregate operational metrics. Protect confidential exports.

Can I install the web app?All

Use the browser's Install app or Add to Home Screen option when supported. An internet connection is still required for live queues.

How do I report a bug?All

Use the report form below. Never include patient names, QR links, account details or confidential information.

Report a bug

Generate a generic report with no free-text or patient data. GitHub will open a draft issue; review it before posting. GitHub issues are public.
