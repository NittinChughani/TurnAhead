Privacy information

This notice describes the current software design, not a certification of compliance. The responsible hospital must approve its own privacy notice, legal basis, retention period and contact information before real use.

Information processed

Staff accounts use Firebase Authentication. Queue records can include patient name/title, doctor, room, ticket number, visit status and operational timestamps. Feedback may include a rating and optional text. Do not enter diagnoses, prescriptions, national ID numbers or other clinical data.

Access and sharing

The public board displays anonymous queue codes. A private QR tracking URL acts as a bearer secret; anyone with the link can see the ticket details. Do not share it publicly. Authorized staff manage live queues; private attendance archives and feedback are restricted to the designated owner.

Storage and retention

Firebase stores operational information. Browser storage remembers theme and notification or feedback preferences. Retention and deletion depend on the hospital's approved policy. Exports must be protected. The optional demo uses fictional in-memory information and sends no queue records to Firebase.

Managed-service commercial records

Subscription requests contain verified business email, organization name, representative and business contact number. Authorized commercial operators review requests and maintain billing records separately from patient records. Do not include payment credentials or patient information in request messages. Organization owners can view their own billing records and request correction, cancellation or export. A retention policy and commercial privacy contact must be approved before launch.

Questions and rights

Contact the responsible hospital for requests about your records. Do not submit private information to public GitHub issues. The hospital must establish an appropriate privacy contact and process before a pilot.
